Privacy notice

This notice explains how Living Africa Holdings (Pty) Ltd handles personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

This website does not collect personal information. There is no contact form, no account and no tracking or analytics. If you email us, we hold your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry.

What we do with it

We use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of the correspondence. We do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone outside the group, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@livingafrica.co.za.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes.
