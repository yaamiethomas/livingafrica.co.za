About Living Africa

We buy land and projects that need work, take them through planning and environmental approval, install services, and then develop them ourselves or sell them ready to build.

How we work

Most of our land has a history: former mining land, historic worker villages and buildings that others had written off.

Our edge is patience and structure. We hold land through the long approvals process and focus on large sites where the value is in getting the rights right.

Our story

Living Africa started in 1999, when the founder and a university friend bought and renovated a house in Johannesburg. Through the early 2000s that grew into a portfolio of apartment blocks and offices across Johannesburg, including buildings in the CBD.

In 2006 the group moved beyond the CBD and focused on large land projects on the East Rand, much of it released by the mining industry. We built up holdings in Ekurhuleni, secured the approvals, and developed or sold them on.

Today the group holds over 500 hectares in Ekurhuleni, is developing hundreds of homes in Reiger Park, Withok and Farrar Park, and is also developing solar infrastructure and a cemetery on the same land platform.

Talk to us about a site or a partnership

We are always looking at land, and we work with partners on development, funding and delivery.
