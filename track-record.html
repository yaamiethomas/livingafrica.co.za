Track record

From a first house in 1999 to land and housing developments across Gauteng.

2024
The Verve, Simbithi, Ballito

Took over 28 apartments and built a new 4,500 sqm A-grade office block. Completed and sold out.

2022
Matholesville, Johannesburg

Over 300 hectares acquired in 2011. Obtained planning and environmental approvals for entry-level housing, then sold.

2020
Angelo Plantation, Boksburg

Redeveloped a historic managers’ village. Built and sold 85 additional units (2018 to 2020).

2016
Delmore Park Ext 8, Boksburg

50 hectares acquired in 2014. Obtained approvals for entry-level housing, then sold.

2014
Comet Village, Boksburg

A historic mine workers’ village of about 260 houses on 43 hectares. Acquired in 2011 for redevelopment, sold in 2014.

2014
139 and 141 Green Way, Greenside

Bought two office blocks, redeveloped 139 into a single office building (completed 2013), and sold.

2012
Comet x14, Boksburg

Developed an industrial estate on the former mine workshops, acquired in 2009.

2008
Move out of the Johannesburg CBD

Sold Cambridge Place, a six-storey office block, and the Protea Court and Branksome Towers apartment buildings (100 units, 2006), and turned to East Rand land.

2006–2008
President Arcade and Corowa, Windsor

Redeveloped, sectionalised and sold 40 apartments with four shops, and a 12-unit block.

1999–2006
Johannesburg residential portfolio

Bought, renovated, rented and sold about 50 apartments across Rosebank, Greenside, Windsor and other suburbs.

1999
First deal, Ferndale, Johannesburg

A house bought, renovated and rented out.
