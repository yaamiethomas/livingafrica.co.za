TEAM

Two principals, one platform.

Living Africa is run day-to-day by its two co-founders, supported by a small professional team that has worked with the group across multiple projects and cycles.

CO-FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

James Thomas

A Chartered Accountant (CA(SA)) with 25+ years in South African property, James leads deal structuring, legal and statutory matters, and group administration across Living Africa's holding company, SPVs and trust structures. He started in property through a Stellenbosch investment club in 1999, and has led the group's East Rand land platform since 2008.

CO-FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Graham Ransom

Graham joined the business in 2011 and has led on-the-ground operations and project execution ever since, working across the group's residential, solar and land projects from Johannesburg. He and James speak multiple times a day across every active workstream.
