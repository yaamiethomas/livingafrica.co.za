WHAT WE DO

Land, residential, solar and income — one platform, one land base.

Living Africa Holdings is built around a single, deep asset: former mining land on Johannesburg's East Rand, assembled and developed over two and a half decades. From that base we run parallel workstreams — municipal land sales, residential township development, utility-scale solar, and income-producing property — each drawing on the same land, relationships and approvals pipeline.

We partner rather than operate everything ourselves: development partners on residential phases, technical partners on solar, specialist operators where the work is operationally heavy. Our role is sourcing, structuring, compliance and oversight across the group.
